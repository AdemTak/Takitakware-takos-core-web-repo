## takOS-core ##

* **takOS core:** takOS core: An **operating system (OS)** with a strong focus on data privacy, serving as the foundation for your IoT devices. The hardware requirements are minimal—a Raspberry Pi with **2 GB of RAM** may even be sufficient. You have the option to add an SSH key, though it is not mandatory. It is an excellent system, though it is currently still in the beta phase; we look forward to hearing your feedback. You can choose how you wish to perform the setup: either via your browser by simply entering "takOS-core.local," or by connecting your Raspberry Pi to a monitor and configuring it directly. There are four options for setting up SSH: first, you can enter your SSH key into a text field; second, you can opt out of using an SSH key and instead create a standard user account (with an optional password); third, you can use your Ubuntu One account by entering your email address and password; and finally, the fourth option allows your Raspberry Pi to automatically retrieve your public key from the internet. Once the setup process concludes, you will be taken to a FreeBSD terminal, where you can enter commands, manage SSH connections, and more. You can also use it to control compatible devices—all powered by the **takOS core** operating system.
